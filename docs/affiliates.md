# Affiliate and referral programs (research only)

Checked November 20, 2026. **Do not apply, sign up, or email anyone from this starter task.** Terms below are what was published on the cited pages that day. Networks change rates; confirm inside the portal after any future application is approved.

These programs fit a household drop-off directory: junk removal when someone cannot haul an item, a truck or dumpster for a cleanout, retail supplies or electronics take-back, and (adjacent) self-storage during a move-out.

## 1. 1-800-GOT-JUNK? (junk removal)

- Official program page: https://www.1800gotjunk.com/us_en/affiliate-program
- Published terms on that page: 2% commission on every completed service; stated average order value of $360; 30-day cookie; creatives available; completed paid jobs are reported through Rakuten Advertising. Brand and trademark bidding is not allowed; the page tells search publishers to add the brand and misspellings as negative keywords. Mobile and tablet bookings on 1800gotjunk.com are eligible.
- Contact route: **application through Rakuten Advertising** (the official page names Rakuten as the program partner). Create a Rakuten publisher account, then apply to the 1-800-GOT-JUNK? program. Start at https://www.rakutenadvertising.com and the official program page above. Transaction questions also go through Rakuten.

## 2. College HUNKS Hauling Junk & Moving (junk removal and moving)

- Official program page: https://www.collegehunkshaulingjunk.com/college-hunks-affiliate-program/
- Published terms on that page: free to join; “earn up to $5 on every lead with no cap”; online application; marketing materials provided after approval. The page says the company has 200+ U.S. locations.
- Other listing, not used as the official rate: a FlexOffers page updated October 17, 2026 lists $16 per move booking lead and $12 per junk removal booking lead, with a 30-day cookie, and also says FlexOffers is “not currently offering this affiliate program in our system” (https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/college-hunks-hauling-junk-moving-affiliate-program/).
- Contact route: **application form on the official program page** (“sign up online”). Confirm the live payout in writing before promoting; the official site and the FlexOffers listing do not agree.

## 3. LoadUp (junk removal)

- Official program page: https://goloadup.com/affiliate/
- Partner signup form: https://goloadup.com/partners/signup/
- Awin merchant profile: https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/89975
- Published terms: no fee to join. Online website referrals run through Awin (banners and links; custom integrations mentioned). The public LoadUp affiliate page does **not** state a commission rate or cookie length. Do not use LoadUp’s separate Brand Builder “15%” figure as the affiliate rate unless LoadUp or Awin says it applies to publishers.
- Contact route: **application form** on the partner signup page (choose the marketing/affiliate option) and/or **apply inside Awin** after creating a free publisher account (Awin’s signup entry is at https://success.awin.com/s/signup-page?language=en_US).

## 4. U-Haul (trucks, trailers, and U-Box)

- Network listing: https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/u-haul-affiliate-program/
- U-Box listing: https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/uhaul-ubox-affiliate-program/
- Published terms on FlexOffers (program page last updated January 24, 2025; listed Active): payout ranges from 2.4% to 4% depending on the product or service; 30-day cookie. The U-Box page lists 4% and the same 30-day cookie. A first-party U-Haul affiliate landing page was not found in this pass; FlexOffers is the published signup path.
- Contact route: **application through FlexOffers**. Create a publisher account at https://www.flexoffers.com/, then apply on the U-Haul program page.

## 5. Penske Truck Rental

- Network listing: https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/penske-truck-rental-affiliate-program/
- Published terms on FlexOffers (updated July 17, 2025; listed Active): 3.2% of sales, “restrictions apply”; 30-day cookie.
- Contact route: **application through FlexOffers**. Create a publisher account, search for “Penske Truck Rental,” and apply.

## 6. Budget Truck Rental

- Official FAQ: https://www.budgettruck.com/resources/affiliatesfaq
- Published terms on that FAQ: free to join; commission is a **variable rate** on completed, paid truck-rental reservations referred from the affiliate link; the exact rate is shown in the affiliate portal and is based on time and mileage; higher rates are negotiable for high performers. Tracking and creatives are on Partnerize. After signup, join the **Avis US** campaign and accept its terms.
- Contact route: **application through Partnerize**, then join the Avis US campaign. If already on Partnerize, the FAQ says to email to be invited into the Avis network. Program questions: abgaffiliateprogram@avisbudget.com. Partnerize setup questions: avis.support@partnerize.com. The FAQ’s “click here to join” button is the signup start; the raw URL was not preserved in the page fetch, so use the FAQ page rather than a guessed Partnerize link.

## 7. The Home Depot (supplies, bags, tools; Bagster is sold at HD)

- Official program page: https://www.homedepot.com/c/SF_MS_The_Home_Depot_Affiliate_Program
- Official FAQ: https://www.homedepot.com/c/SF_MS_Affiliate_Program_FAQs
- Network listing: https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/home-depot-affiliate-program/
- Published terms on FlexOffers (updated January 24, 2025; listed Active): 0.8% of sales, including appliances and small electrics; 6.4% for HD Home products; **1-day cookie**.
- The official Home Depot program and FAQ pages did not successfully load in this research pass (HTTP 500 and a timeout), so current first-party rates were not confirmed here. Some 2026 recap articles still quote 1% / 8% with a 24-hour cookie; treat those as unverified until the official page is readable.
- Contact route: **application through FlexOffers** and/or the official Home Depot affiliate page. Do not apply until those official terms are re-checked.

## 8. Best Buy (electronics; relevant on TV and e-waste pages)

- Official program page: https://www.bestbuy.com/site/misc/best-buy-affiliate-program/pcmcat198500050002.c?id=pcmcat198500050002
- Published terms on that page (fetched November 20, 2026): creators and publishers can earn commissions by sharing product links from a website, blog, or social account; the page advertises tracking tools and storefronts. **It does not state a commission percentage or cookie length on the public page.**
- Third-party recaps describe the U.S. program as running on Impact, with category rates often around 0.5–1% and a short (about one-day) window, and some electronics categories at 0%. Those figures were not confirmed on Best Buy’s own page in this pass.
- Contact route: **application from the official affiliate page** (the page is the published start; 2026 recaps say you then apply to the Best Buy US campaign in Impact as a publisher).

## 9. Amazon Associates (secondary: bags, straps, covers, not the core listing)

- Official signup: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/welcome
- Operating policies: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies/
- Published requirements, from Amazon’s Associates help as commonly cited: a qualifying public website or app with original content; new U.S. associates must generate a minimum number of qualifying sales within a stated window (Amazon’s help has listed three qualifying sales in 180 days; personal orders do not count). Commission varies by product category; use Amazon’s current commission table in Associates Central rather than a blog summary. Payment timing is set in Amazon’s policies.
- Contact route: **application form** on Associates Central (https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/welcome). Approval is through Amazon, not a one-off email pitch.

## 10. SpareFoot (adjacent: storage during a cleanout or move)

- Network listing: https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/sparefoot-affiliate-program/
- Published terms on FlexOffers (updated September 3, 2026; listed Active): **$36 per confirmed move-in**; reservation leads are excluded; 30-day cookie.
- Another network listing (Takeads) describes “up to $4.20 per confirmed lead,” which does not match the FlexOffers move-in CPA. If this program is ever used, confirm the live offer in the portal you actually join.
- Contact route: **application through FlexOffers** (publisher account, then apply to SpareFoot). Storable’s consumer marketplaces (SpareFoot, Storage.com, SelfStorage.com) have also been described as affiliate-managed by Hamster Garage; that is background, not a second official signup URL.
